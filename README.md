## Interesting Article

[Why Rust is the most loved language among developers - Stack Overflow 2025 Survey](https://stackoverflow.com/blog/2025/developer-survey)

I find this article very interesting because it shows how Rust has consistently ranked as the most loved programming language for many years.  
What attracts me most is its focus on **memory safety without garbage collection**, zero-cost abstractions, and excellent performance — qualities that make it suitable for systems programming, WebAssembly, embedded devices, and even gradually replacing parts of C/C++ code in large projects.

The survey also highlights the growing adoption in infrastructure, blockchain, and game development, which aligns with where I want to focus my career.
